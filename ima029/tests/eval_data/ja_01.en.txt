A Father's Love

When my father was young, he served as a soldier. My mother often said that he had never held a child in his arms and had never attended a parent-teacher meeting. Among all those things he had supposedly never done, my father always seemed unsure of what to say. Perhaps he had not been very interested in us.

But one day, my father suddenly told me something. He said that on the day I was born, he was so excited that he ran out to buy a bright red alarm clock. Ever since then, whenever I hear the words “not even once,” that red clock comes to mind.

After I started working, there were times when I returned home late at night. On those occasions, I sometimes saw my father waiting for me at a dark street corner. As the years passed, I started my own family and moved beyond my father's protection. Yet when I walk alone at night, I sometimes feel the warmth of someone worrying about me.
