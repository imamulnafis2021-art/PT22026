Expanded meaning
“In the kingdom of hunger, the world is prose; the full moon seems like a scorched flatbread.”

Main idea: The feeling of hunger is intense and overwhelming. The cruel blows of poverty can destroy people's ability to see beauty and feel love. Therefore, satisfying hunger is essential.

Expanded explanation: Imagination and reality are not the same. People are naturally imaginative, and many things in nature can attract the mind. But hunger makes people suffer; when hunger is relieved, they can once again become absorbed in the world. To a hungry person, the world seems as dull as prose. The beauty and charm of the full moon cannot bring such a person joy. Family and social hardships leave people distressed and exhausted, and familiar pleasures of the world become dim.

Comment: The first need in human life is to satisfy hunger. If that is difficult, all the joys of life disappear. The full moon can seem like a scorched flatbread.
