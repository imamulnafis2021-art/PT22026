Long ago, I imagined many scenes of the future. I imagined us being together for a very long time, and I also imagined what it would be like when one day I finally gave you up.

I thought that one clear morning, when I woke up with sunlight filling the room, I would suddenly discover that I no longer loved you, and then begin my new life.

But I discovered that I was wrong.

In fact, after I had begun a new life, somewhere in the quiet passage of time, an ordinary moment arrived when I suddenly realized that I had already forgotten you for a long time.

That sudden realization was melancholy and made me feel desolate. I used to believe that my feelings for you were great, irreplaceable, and an indispensable part of my life. I wondered that if one day those feelings disappeared, their disappearance would surely be a dramatic event. Yet in reality, they faded away silently.

I truly loved you once, with all sincerity. I think I may never experience that kind of love a second time in my life. Even the slightest change in your mood could make my tears flow like a river.
