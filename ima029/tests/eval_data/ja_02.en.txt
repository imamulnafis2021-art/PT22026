I went to Osaka. Osaka is a city in Japan and is a very lively and enjoyable place. First, I visited Tsutenkaku. Tsutenkaku is one of Osaka's symbols, and the view from the tall tower is wonderful.

Next, I went to Dotonbori. Dotonbori is a famous tourist area with many restaurants and shops. After that, I visited Osaka Castle. Osaka Castle is a historic building, and it also has a large park.

I also enjoyed Osaka's delicious food. There were many tasty dishes, such as takoyaki, okonomiyaki, and ramen.

Osaka is truly a fun place, and I was able to make many wonderful memories.
