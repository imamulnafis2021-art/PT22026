I came to understand that if your faith simply means looking back at the cross and concluding that you have escaped the snare, it is still possible to keep holding on to resentment. I was led to understand more deeply what true faith is: being satisfied with everything God has done in Jesus. It is not merely looking back at the past to discover that you have escaped a trap; rather, it looks back in order to see and taste God, who grants a future fulfilled by an ever-growing reconciliation and fellowship with Him.
